Cookies Policy
last revised · may 2026

Prymora Junebriar uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience, analyse site traffic, and personalise content. This policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and your choices.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your actions and preferences (such as language, font size, and other display settings) over a period of time, so you don’t have to keep re-entering them whenever you come back to the site or browse from one page to another.

🍪 Our approach: We use only necessary, functional, and analytical cookies. We never use intrusive tracking or sell your data. Your privacy is at the heart of our journal.

2. Types of cookies we use

We classify cookies into the following categories:

  • Strictly necessary: essential for site functionality (e.g., logging in, cart management).
  • Performance / analytics: collect anonymised data about how visitors use our site (e.g., Google Analytics) so we can improve.
  • Functionality: remember your preferences (e.g., theme, language).
  • Targeting / advertising: we do not use these – no behavioural ads.

3. How we use cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Ensure the site works correctly and securely.
  • Understand visitor engagement and improve content.
  • Remember your settings (e.g., dark/light mode, if applicable).
  • Provide a seamless, editorial reading experience.

4. Your control and consent

When you first visit Prymora Junebriar, you will see a cookie banner allowing you to accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can change your preferences at any time via the link in our footer or by adjusting your browser settings.

  • Browser settings: most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Instructions for Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge are available.
  • Opt-out: you can disable Google Analytics tracking via the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
🍪 Cookie preferences at a glance Essential: always on · Analytics: you choose · Marketing: never used

5. Third-party cookies

We use a limited number of trusted third-party services (e.g., Google Analytics, font providers) that may set cookies. These are used to improve functionality and measure performance. We do not allow third-party advertising cookies.

6. Updates to this policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology or legislation. The latest version will always be posted here with the revision date. Please check back periodically.
