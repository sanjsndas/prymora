Privacy Policy
last revised · may 2026

Prymora Junebriar respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our journal.

1. Information we collect

We may collect the following types of data:

  • Personal identification: name, email address, and any details you provide via contact forms or newsletter sign-ups.
  • Usage data: pages visited, time spent, and referring URLs, collected via analytics tools (anonymized where possible).
  • Cookies & tracking: small files stored on your device to enhance user experience (see our Cookie Policy).

2. How we use your information

Your data helps us:

  • Deliver editorial updates and exclusive gardening insights.
  • Respond to inquiries and provide customer support.
  • Improve our content, layout, and user experience.
  • Comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud.
Our promise: We never sell or rent your personal data to third parties. Any sharing is strictly for operational purposes (e.g., email delivery services) and always under confidentiality agreements.

3. Legal basis for processing

We process your data under the following lawful bases:

  • Consent: when you opt-in to receive communications.
  • Contractual necessity: to fulfill a service you request.
  • Legitimate interest: to improve our journal and protect against misuse.

4. Data retention

We keep your personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined. For example, newsletter data is retained until you unsubscribe. Usage analytics are aggregated and anonymized after 24 months.

Data typeRetention period
Newsletter subscriber infoUntil unsubscription
Contact form messages2 years
Analytics (anonymized)24 months

5. Your rights

Under applicable privacy laws, you have the right to:

  • Access – request a copy of your data.
  • Rectification – correct inaccurate information.
  • Erasure – ask us to delete your data.
  • Restrict – limit processing in certain cases.
  • Object – opt-out of marketing communications.

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@prymorajunebriar.com.

6. Security measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encryption, regular security audits, and restricted access to personal information.

7. Third-party links

Our journal may contain links to external sites (e.g., seed suppliers, botanical archives). We are not responsible for their privacy practices. We encourage you to review their policies before providing data.

8. Children's privacy

Prymora Junebriar is not directed at individuals under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us immediately.

9. Updates to this policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted here, with the revision date at the top. Significant changes will be communicated via email or site notice.
